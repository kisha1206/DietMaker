# Diet Maker

A web app that builds a personal diet plan. Enter your weight, height, age, gender, activity level, food preference (veg, eggetarian or non-veg), goal (gaining, cutting or recomposing), protein powder intake, preferred protein sources and whether you eat at a hostel mess or at home. It gives you:

- daily calorie, protein, carb and fat targets (Mifflin-St Jeor formula)
- a 7-day Indian meal plan with portions scaled to your targets, and a Swap button on every meal
- hostel-friendly and home-cooked meal options
- a PDF export of the full week

Everything runs in the browser from a single `index.html`; there is no build step.

## Run locally

Open `index.html` in a browser.
